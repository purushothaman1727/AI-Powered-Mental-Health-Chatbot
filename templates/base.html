🚨 Immediate Help & Support

If you are in distress, feeling overwhelmed, or thinking of self-harm, please know that you are not alone. Reach out to one of the helplines below:

📞 India Support Lines
  • iCall: 022-25521111 (Mon-Sat: 8am - 10pm)
  • AASRA: 91-9820466726 (24/7 Helpline)
  • Vandrevala Foundation: 9999 666 555 (24/7 Helpline)
📞 International Support Lines
  • US: Call or text 988 to reach Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
  • UK: Call 111 (NHS) or 116 123 (Samaritans)
  • Worldwide: Visit befrienders.org to find support in your area
